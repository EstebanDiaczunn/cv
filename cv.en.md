## Profile

I've spent three years building backend systems for healthcare in Argentina. I connected e-prescriptions to five national providers and the Ministry of Health; thousands go through every day. On my own, I built and run Clara, a WhatsApp assistant that serves patients and books appointments for clinics.

I build by directing AI agents: I write the spec, review every change, verify, and ship. I published NAIKI, the code review standard I use for it.
